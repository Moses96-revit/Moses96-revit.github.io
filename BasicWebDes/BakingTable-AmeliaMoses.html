Baking Ingredients and Tools

Common Baking Measurements
Ingredient Volume (Cups) Weight (Grams) Notes & Density
Flour (AP) 1 cup 120g Sifted weight may vary. Medium
Granulated Sugar 1 cup 200g Consistent weight. High
Butter 1 cup (2 sticks) 227g Can be measured by stick. High
Water 1 cup 236g Standard density reference.

Essential Baking Tools Checklist

  • Measuring cups and spoons
  • Mixing bowls (various sizes)
  • Whisk and spatula

Baking Steps (Ordered List)

  1. Preheat oven to required temperature.
  2. Mix dry ingredients in one bowl.
  3. Mix wet ingredients separately, then combine.
