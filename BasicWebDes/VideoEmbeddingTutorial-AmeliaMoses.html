How to Embed a YouTube Video

A step-by-step guide for web developers.

Step 1: Locate the Share Button

Navigate to the YouTube video you wish to use. Look directly beneath the video player and click on the "Share" button located next to the Like/Dislike icons.

Step 2: Select the Embed Option

A pop-up menu will appear with various social sharing options. Click on the "Embed" icon (represented by the < > symbols) at the far left of the list.

Step 3: Copy the IFrame Code

A panel will open on the right showing the HTML code. Ensure "Enable privacy-enhanced mode" is checked if desired, then click the "Copy" button in the bottom right corner. Paste this code into your HTML file.

Final Result: Embedded Video
